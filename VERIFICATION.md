## Limits of these checks

The smoke exercise tests synthetic artifacts and the audit helper; it is not an independent end-to-end model evaluation or a universal document converter test. PDF, Office, OCR and media processing must be tested against the tools and real source formats available in the destination workspace. Source coverage, substantive accuracy and heading-level retrieval require the builder's additional checks.

No real project corpus was ingested, no accounts were connected, and no recurring routine was activated for this release. The bundle is usable by reading its files directly. Installation and app discovery on another computer remain separate checks.
